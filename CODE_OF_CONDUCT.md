# Code of conduct

This code applies to everyone taking part in Examen Silentii: authors, reviewers, editors, maintainers and anyone commenting in its repositories.

## Expected conduct

- Discuss the work, not the person. Criticism should be specific, accurate and aimed at software, data, methods or claims.
- Assume good faith. Errors in software and data are common and usually honest.
- Be courteous to maintainers, whose work is the subject of examination and who often maintain it without pay.
- Declare conflicts of interest.
- Respect the journal's rules on responsible disclosure.

## Unacceptable conduct

- Personal attacks, insults, ridicule or harassment.
- Discriminatory language or behaviour of any kind.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Deliberately misrepresenting someone's work or words.
- Pressuring reviewers, editors or maintainers outside the open process.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a concern privately to an editor, using the contact details on the [Editors page](https://examen-silentii.github.io/editors/). The editors will respond promptly and confidentially. Comments that breach this code may be hidden or removed, and people who breach it repeatedly or seriously may be barred from taking part in the journal.
